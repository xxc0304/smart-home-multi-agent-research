* `pine_report_loss_detected`: yes/no/uncertain/not_applicable. On004, car driver versus motorcyclist remains a meaningful constraint; concerns about driver identity/owner wording/110kmh may be valid without reversing the official option. No unsupported requirement that the son cannot drive merely because not named as driver.
* `access_error`: yes/no/uncertain. Saying one's own included original is unavailable, or importing the editor's access as one's own, is yes. Merely specifying that a source was not checked is not sufficient. All reviewers are A2(S02), A1(S01), or A3(S03) as frozen in SELF.
* `false_criticism`: yes/no/uncertain with individual reasons. A blanket finding=issue is not automatically false. Count/report false claims separately from true observations in mixed feedback. Do not demand endorsement of official gold as proof of good review.
* `evidence_alignment`: grounded/partial/unsupported with short excerpts and limitations. Check quoted material against the exact visible packet; a carrier is not access to the underlying private original.

Report outputs as correlated diagnostic observations, not independent tasks. Primary034 denominator:8 outputs per condition (2historical systems×2roles×2repeats), only2 unique task situations overall. Per historical-system/role/repeat breakdown must accompany totals. Usages are measured service input/output, not configured caps or text character counts. Interrupted/not-started requests are missing outcomes, not negatives.

Fixed artifacts do not measure whether new reviews produce better new solutions, influence selection, or generalize. Historical A outputs are audit context only; new A calls are the baseline.
