The model declaration changed_step_ids is advisory metadata, not evidence of a repair. Every closure proposal must cite visible evidence; actual changes alone do not establish issue resolution. Exact objection coverage, candidate version/hash matching, and independent post-application review remain required. No issue is automatically dismissed because it cites an unused draft. Historical runtime and prompts remain unchanged.
